    Google Sheets sync

    Pre-filled with the team's shared sheet, so sync works out of the box for everyone — no setup needed. Sync buttons push your current driver(s) to the sheet, then pull back anything your teammates have synced — new drivers appear automatically, and item statuses catch up to the shared sheet. To point at a different sheet, paste your own deployed Google Apps Script web app URL (see the README) and Save; clear the field to keep this device fully local instead.

    Backup & restore

    All data lives only in this browser. Export a backup regularly, especially before clearing browser data.
